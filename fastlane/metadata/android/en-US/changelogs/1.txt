Initial release.

- Connect to FTMS-compatible treadmills and indoor bikes over Bluetooth
- BH Fitness iConcept support — treadmills, indoor bikes and vertical bikes (C01 models)
- Heart rate sensor support via BLE (Gadgetbridge compatible) — use a smart band or chest strap as HR sensor
- HR-only mode: start and record workouts with just a heart rate sensor, no FTMS machine required
- Real-time dashboard with speed, cadence, power, distance, heart rate, energy, time, inclination and resistance
- Session recording starts and stops automatically when the machine starts or stops
- Session summary (avg/max speed, power, HR) saved on workout completion
- Workout History screen listing all past sessions with machine type, date, duration, distance and avg HR
- Export workouts as FIT or CSV from the History screen
- FIT export includes per-second records with speed, cadence, power, HR, distance, grade and resistance
- Cross Trainer (elliptical) and Stair Climber / Step Climber machine type recognition with correct FIT sport
- Device info (MAC address, serial number, hardware and firmware revision) shown when connected
- Previously connected devices remain visible in the scan list after disconnect, with a Reconnect button
- Bonded Gadgetbridge devices (smart bands) are shown in the scan list even when not actively advertising
- Audio peripherals (headsets, earbuds, speakers) are excluded from the scan list
- Notification shown when a device unexpectedly disconnects
- Debug Bluetooth communication logging saved to Documents/FTMSBridge/debug/
- Portrait and landscape layouts
- About menu entry showing the current app version
